Power settings, one switch

Keep your Mac available when work needs it.

Switch between Server and Normal power modes from the menu bar. Preview the changes, authorize them, and keep a backup you can restore.

Apple Silicon · arm64 macOS 15 minimum

A small menu bar utility for Macs that sometimes need to stay awake and sometimes should sleep normally.

Two clear modes

Stay available. Return to everyday settings.

PowerModeManager groups supported macOS power settings into two profiles, so you can change them without remembering individual `pmset` values.

01

Server Mode

Designed to keep system sleep off while a Mac is serving files, builds, or other work. The display can still sleep. Settings depend on what macOS exposes for your Mac and power source.

02

Normal Mode

Return to the app’s everyday sleep profile when the Mac no longer needs to stay available. Review the proposed values before you apply either mode.

Inside the app

See the modes before you switch.

The English interface shows the two profiles and the current mode. The selected mode reflects the Mac running the app and may differ on your Mac.

Control and recovery

Know what changes before you apply them.

Power settings can affect sleep, heat, and battery use. The app keeps the change visible and gives you a way back.

Preview first

Compare the profile values with the settings available on your Mac before applying a mode.

Authorize changes

macOS asks for administrator authorization when the app needs to change system power settings.

Back up and restore

The app saves a local settings backup before applying changes and provides a restore path.

!

Use Server Mode deliberately. It disables system sleep in its profile and can increase power use and battery drain on a MacBook. Keep a laptop connected to power, provide ventilation, and restore Normal Mode when continuous availability is no longer needed. PowerModeManager does not limit battery charging or stop charging.

Download the installer and drag PowerModeManager into Applications.

Review before changing

Open the menu bar app, inspect the proposed settings, and approve only the changes you want.

Quit from the menu

Closing the main window leaves the menu bar app running. Choose Quit from its menu to exit.

Update or remove

Updates are manual. To uninstall, remove the app from Applications. Backups and optional logs remain on the Mac until you delete them.

Privacy and support

Your settings stay on your Mac.

The app has no analytics, account, or user-data transfer feature. This static website also uses no analytics scripts.

Local files

  • Backups: ~/.powermode-backups
  • Optional monitoring log: ~/.powermode-monitor.log
  • Diagnostic reports may contain your Mac model, host name, and power settings.

These files may remain after uninstall. Review a report before sharing it. Read the full privacy notice.

Support and licensing

Report problems in GitHub Issues in English or Korean. Include app version, macOS version, Mac model, and steps to reproduce. Do not attach raw diagnostics unless you have reviewed them and removed private details.

For a suspected security issue, use private vulnerability reporting. The app is free under the MIT License; its terms allow redistribution and sale.
